Move-Out Cleaning in Hickory: The Checklist That Gets Your Deposit Back

The room-by-room checklist landlords actually check — so nothing gets left for the final walkthrough to find.

A move-out clean gets your deposit back when it covers what landlords and property managers actually inspect: the inside of the oven and refrigerator, inside cabinets and drawers, scrubbed bathrooms, and floors finished clean enough to photograph. Most deposit deductions for cleaning come from a short list of missed spots — this checklist walks them room by room, so nothing gets left for the final walkthrough to find.

Whether you’re a renter handing back keys in Hickory or a landlord turning a home between tenants, this is the standard the final inspection is really measuring.

What Landlords Actually Check

A landlord’s final walkthrough isn’t random. It follows the same trouble spots in nearly every home, because those are the spots tenants most often leave behind:

  • Inside the oven and refrigerator — opened, every time
  • Inside cabinets, drawers, and closets — wiped down, not just closed
  • Bathroom surfaces — tub, shower, toilet, and fixtures, scrubbed and disinfected
  • Floors — vacuumed and mopped throughout, ready for listing photos
  • The details — windowsills, cabinet fronts, light fixtures, trash fully out

If a spot can be opened, a landlord opens it. A move-out clean has to be built for an empty home where nothing hides behind furniture.

The Room-by-Room Move-Out Checklist

Kitchen — where deposits are won and lost

Degrease the stovetop. Scrub and disinfect the sink and countertops. Clean appliance exteriors — and then the part most people skip: the oven cleaned inside and out, the refrigerator cleaned inside, and the microwave inside and out. Wet-clean the cabinet fronts, then wipe out every cabinet and drawer interior. Finish with the floors.

Bathrooms

Tub and shower scrubbed, disinfected, and rinsed. Toilet disinfected inside and out. Sink, countertops, and mirrors cleaned; chrome fixtures shined. Cabinet fronts wet-cleaned and interiors wiped. Floors disinfected — bathroom floors are one of the first things an inspector looks at up close.

Bedrooms and closets

Vacuum or wash floors, including closet floors. Wipe closet shelves and rods. Clean windowsills, dust light fixtures and ceiling fans, and remove fingerprints from doorframes and switch plates. Empty closets show every scuff and crumb — there’s nowhere for dirt to hide.

Living areas and the whole home

Vacuum and mop every floor. Clean windowsills and doors, remove cobwebs from ceilings and vents, and dust baseboards. Take out all trash — a single forgotten bag reads as “not finished” in an inspection.

When to Book a Move-Out Clean

Book the clean after the home is fully empty and before your final walkthrough or key handover. Cleaning around boxes and furniture leaves the exact spots inspectors check — under, behind, and inside — unreachable. If your lease ends on a Friday, the clean belongs between the last load out and the walkthrough, not after. When you book with us, move-in and move-out cleans are paid in full up front, so the date is locked and the price is settled before moving day arrives — one less thing in motion during the move.

Do It Yourself or Hire It Out? An Honest Word

If you have the time, the supplies, and a small home that’s been kept up, you can work this checklist yourself — set aside most of a day, go room by room, and don’t skip the insides. Plenty of people do, and done thoroughly, it works.

Hire it out when time is the thing you’re short on — and during a move, it usually is. Hire it out when the home is large, when the kitchen carries real grease buildup, or when your deposit is big enough that a deduction would sting more than a cleaning would. And if you’re a landlord or property manager turning a home between tenants, a professional clean gives you a documented standard: the home was Reset, top to bottom, before the new keys changed hands.

Either way, judge the result the same way your landlord will: open everything, look closely, and check the floors last.
